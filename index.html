AboutThe Foundry

Framework Foundry LLC is an independent game studio built on a single conviction: the best games are ones that feel genuinely alive. We use AI not as a gimmick, but as a core design tool — enabling narratives that react, worlds that adapt, and characters that remember.

We're a small, focused studio. We ship when things are ready, we cut what doesn't serve the player, and we build experiences we'd want to play ourselves.

Our first title, Ad-friend-ture, is available now on iOS. More is being forged.
